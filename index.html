01 · Traditional

Sumi-e

Sumi-e (墨絵) is the traditional Japanese art of ink wash painting: capturing the spirit of a subject in as few brushstrokes as possible, where the empty space matters as much as the ink. Each piece is signed with my carved seal, ヤナ.

Painted with the traditional tools: the ink stick (sumi 墨), ground with water on an ink stone (suzuri 硯), and bamboo handled brushes (fude 筆).
